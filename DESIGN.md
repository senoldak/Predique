# Design System: Predique Minimalist Trading Terminal

## 1. Design Philosophy & Mode
- **Mode:** Operate (High-frequency professional trading environment).
- **Aesthetic:** Minimalist Bloomberg/Trading Terminal.
- **Principles:**
  - Utilitarian precision: zero fluff, crisp 1px hairline borders.
  - High information density with breathing room: compact data tables, clean numbers, tabular numerals.
  - Subdued, matte dark background with high-contrast readable typography.
  - Functional accents: Emerald (#10b981) for gains/connected, Rose (#ef4444) for loss/error, Amber (#f59e0b) for active focus.

## 2. Color Palette & Tokens
- **Background Root:** `#080a0f` (Deep matte obsidian)
- **Panel Surface:** `#0e1219` (Subtle dark graphite)
- **Panel Border:** `#1a202c` (Crisp 1px border)
- **Border Highlight:** `#2d3748` (On hover/active)
- **Text Primary:** `#f8fafc` (High contrast pure slate)
- **Text Secondary:** `#94a3b8` (Muted silver)
- **Text Dim:** `#64748b` (Subdued caption)
- **Accent Active:** `#f59e0b` (Amber gold for key action & bee identity)
- **Positive / Profit:** `#10b981` (Muted terminal emerald)
- **Negative / Loss:** `#ef4444` (Terminal crimson)
- **Code / Mono Font:** `'JetBrains Mono', monospace`
- **Interface Font:** `'Space Grotesk', -apple-system, sans-serif`
